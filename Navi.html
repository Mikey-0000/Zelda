Navi - The Fairy Companion

Navi is the fairy companion of Link in The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Sent by the Great Deku Tree to the boy who had no fairy, she becomes his guide, offering hints and advice on how to progress. Her catchphrase, "Hey! Listen!", has become part of the game's legacy.

How Navi Helps

  • She glows blue as she flutters beside Link, and turns yellow when something nearby has caught her attention.
  • She powers Z-targeting: lock onto an enemy or a person and Link keeps them in view.
  • She offers hints about enemies, puzzles and where to go next.
  • At the end of the adventure, she says goodbye to Link and flies away.

Other Zelda Companions

  • Tatl, the fairy who joins Link in Majora's Mask.
  • Midna, who helps Link in Twilight Princess.
  • Fi, the spirit of the sword in Skyward Sword.

The glowing fairy following your cursor is Navi too. Click her for a hint.

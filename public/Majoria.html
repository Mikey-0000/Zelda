The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask

Majora's Mask is an action-adventure game released in 2000 for the Nintendo 64 as the direct follow-up to Ocarina of Time. Link, searching for a lost friend, is drawn into the parallel world of Termina, where a mischievous Skull Kid wearing a cursed mask has set the moon on a collision course with Clock Town.

Three Days

The game runs on a three-day countdown. Link must play the Song of Time to return to the dawn of the first day, keeping what he has learned and resetting the world. Residents of Clock Town follow their own schedules, and helping them is as important as clearing dungeons.

Masks

Masks are the game's signature feature. Some turn Link into a different being, while others give small abilities. Tap one to learn what it does.

Termina

  • Clock Town, the central hub, with its giant clock tower.
  • Woodfall, Snowhead, Great Bay and Stone Tower: the four regions beyond the town.
  • The Happy Mask Salesman, a strange merchant with a connection to the cursed mask.
  • Tatl, a fairy who accompanies Link through the game.

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild

Breath of the Wild is an open-world adventure released in 2017 for the Nintendo Switch and Wii U. Link wakes in the Shrine of Resurrection after a long sleep and must free Hyrule from Calamity Ganon, with the help of the memories and spirits of the Champions.

A Kingdom Reborn

  • An open world you can climb, glide over and explore in almost any order.
  • The Sheikah Slate, a tablet that gives Link abilities and a map.
  • 120 shrines and hundreds of Korok seeds hidden across Hyrule.
  • Four Divine Beasts: Vah Ruta, Vah Rudania, Vah Medoh and Vah Naboris.
  • Four Champions: Mipha, Daruk, Revali and Urbosa.

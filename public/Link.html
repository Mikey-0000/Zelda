Link - The Hero of Hyrule

CLICK · HOVER

Link is the protagonist of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, a young boy who embarks on a quest to save the land of Hyrule from the evil king Ganondorf.

Throughout the game, Link is guided by the fairy companion Navi, who provides hints and advice on how to progress through the story. Link's journey takes him through various environments, including forests, mountains, and dungeons, where he must solve puzzles, defeat enemies, and uncover the secrets of Hyrule's history.

Link is known for his bravery, determination, and selflessness, as he faces numerous challenges and battles formidable foes to protect the land and its inhabitants. His iconic green tunic, pointed hat, and the Master Sword have become symbols of his heroism and courage.

Link's Journey

Link's journey begins in Kokiri Forest, where he is raised as a child among the Kokiri, a race of people who never grow up. He is the only one without a guardian fairy, which makes him feel like an outsider, until the fairy Navi is sent to him by the Great Deku Tree. The Deku Tree, who is dying from a curse, tells Link that an evil man is threatening Hyrule, and he sends him out into the world to stop it. After the Deku Tree dies, Link leaves the forest for the first time and travels to Hyrule Castle.

There he meets Princess Zelda, who has had a prophetic dream about a dark storm and the rising power of Ganondorf, the leader of the Gerudo. She asks Link to collect the three Spiritual Stones, which are needed to open the Door of Time and reach the Triforce before Ganondorf can. Link earns the stones by helping the three peoples of Hyrule: the Kokiri's Emerald from the Great Deku Tree, the Goron's Ruby from Dodongo's Cavern, and the Zora's Sapphire from Jabu-Jabu's Belly.

With the stones in hand, Link returns to the Temple of Time and pulls the Master Sword from its pedestal. Doing so seals him away for seven years, because he is too young to be the Hero of Time. In the meantime, Ganondorf uses the opened Door of Time to seize the Triforce of Power and conquers Hyrule, turning it into a wasteland.

When Link wakes as an adult, he learns that he must awaken six Sages to seal Ganondorf away. He travels through the Forest, Fire, Water, Shadow, and Spirit Temples, with help from allies such as Sheik, who is really Zelda in disguise. Link can also travel back and forth in time by returning the Master Sword to its pedestal, which lets him use abilities and items from both his childhood and adulthood to solve puzzles.

Once the Sages are awakened, Zelda is revealed to be the final Sage, but Ganondorf captures her just before the final confrontation. Link storms Ganon's Castle, fights through its trials, and defeats Ganondorf, who then transforms into the monstrous Ganon. Link defeats him with the help of Zelda and the Master Sword, and the Sages seal Ganon in the Sacred Realm. Zelda sends Link back to his childhood so he can live the years he lost, and he returns to the time before the war began.

Quest Items

The three Spiritual Stones are in your inventory. Earn them by exploring, playing songs, and finding Skulltulas.

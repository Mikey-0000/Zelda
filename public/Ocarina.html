The Ocarina of Time

The ocarina is a small wind instrument at the heart of Ocarina of Time. Link first receives the Fairy Ocarina from his friend Saria, and later the Ocarina of Time, entrusted to him by Princess Zelda. By playing short melodies he learns along the way, Link can call his horse, change the time of day, summon storms and open the way forward.

Play It Yourself

Play the six-note songs. Each new song you discover earns a Piece of Heart, and some of them change the world around you.

Songs

  • Zelda's Lullaby: taught by Impa. Rains hearts.
  • Epona's Song: taught by Malon. A horse runs past.
  • Saria's Song: taught by Saria. Leaves drift down.
  • Sun's Song: taught by the Composer Brothers. Switches day and night.
  • Song of Time: taught by Zelda. Time stands still for a moment.
  • Song of Storms: taught by Guru-Guru. Brings rain and thunder.

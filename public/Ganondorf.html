Ganondorf - The King of Evil

Ganondorf is the long-standing villain of the Zelda series. In Ocarina of Time he is the leader of the Gerudo, a tribe of desert thieves, and the only male of his kind born in a hundred years. Appearing at first as a loyal servant, he schemes to seize the Triforce and rule Hyrule.

The Triforce of Power

By following Link into the Sacred Realm, Ganondorf lays hands on the Triforce and claims the Triforce of Power. The sacred relic splits into three, with Zelda and Link keeping the Triforces of Wisdom and Courage. Armed with the Triforce of Power, he conquers Hyrule and becomes the monstrous Ganon.

Across the Series

  • He returns in many games, often as Ganon or as Ganondorf.
  • In Twilight Princess, his ambitions return to threaten Hyrule again.
  • In Breath of the Wild, his malice lives on as Calamity Ganon.

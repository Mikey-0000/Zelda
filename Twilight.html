The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess

Twilight Princess is a 2006 action-adventure game for the GameCube and Wii. Link is a ranch hand in the peaceful Ordon Village until a shadowy force draws him into a darker version of Hyrule, where he takes the form of a wolf.

Wolf Link

In the Twilight Realm, Link becomes a wolf with heightened senses. Wolf Link can dig, track scents and see spirits.

Characters

  • Midna, an imp-like being of the Twilight Realm who becomes Link's guide and ally.
  • Princess Zelda, who rules a Hyrule threatened by the encroaching twilight.
  • Zant, the usurper who rules the Twilight Realm and wants to conquer Hyrule.
  • Ganondorf, the Gerudo king whose ambition returns to threaten the land.

The Story in Brief

Link sets out to rescue the children of Ordon Village and uncover the source of the twilight. Along the way he works with Midna to restore the Fused Shadows and the Mirror of Twilight, learning that the fate of both worlds hangs on the choices of a few.

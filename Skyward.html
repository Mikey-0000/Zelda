The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword

Skyward Sword is a 2011 action-adventure game for the Wii. Set earlier in the series timeline than any other game, it follows a young Link and Zelda from Skyloft, a town on islands floating above the clouds, and tells how the Master Sword came to be.

Above the Clouds

Skyloft's residents ride giant Loftwings through the sky and dive down to the surface world.

Key Ideas

  • Motion controls: Link swings his sword in the direction the player swings the Wii Remote.
  • Fi, the spirit inside the sword, who guides Link.
  • Ghirahim, a flamboyant villain who serves the demon king Demise.
  • The Master Sword is forged here, and the long chain of events leading to Ganondorf begins.
